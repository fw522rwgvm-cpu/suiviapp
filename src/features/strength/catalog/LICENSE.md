# Exercise catalogue — provenance and licence

876 exercises and 1746 photographs come from
**free-exercise-db** — <https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db>.

## Licence

free-exercise-db is released under **the Unlicense**, a dedication of the work
to the public domain. The full text is in `LICENSE.md` at the root of that
repository; its operative sentence is:

> This is free and unencumbered software released into the public domain.
> Anyone is free to copy, modify, publish, use, compile, sell, or distribute
> this software, either in source code form or as a compiled binary, for any
> purpose, commercial or non-commercial, and by any means.

No attribution is required. This file exists anyway, because where a thing came
from is worth recording even when nobody is owed a credit.

## What is NOT from the source

The **French names** are this project's own work. free-exercise-db is English
only. See `scripts/exercise-names-fr.mjs`.

The photographs are **resized** — 400 px wide, JPEG quality
65 — from the originals, which are around 850 px. Nothing else is
altered.

## A reservation worth writing down

The photographs show an identifiable person in a gym. The Unlicense is a
copyright dedication, and a copyright dedication is not a model release: it
settles who may copy the file, not what the person in it agreed to. This was
raised and the decision to use them was taken deliberately, with that
distinction understood. It is recorded here so the decision stays visible rather
than becoming an assumption.
